# Bundled molecular sources

`template/raw/HOH_ideal.sdf` and `template/raw/TYR_ideal.sdf` are unchanged individual ideal-coordinate records from the wwPDB Chemical Component Dictionary, retained from GX-TXT's existing `viewer.molecule_3d/library`. That library records retrieval on 2026-10-01 UTC and declares CC0-1.0 under the PDB archive usage policy, https://www.rcsb.org/pages/usage-policy. The exact download URLs and SHA-256 values are preserved in `template/reference/molecular_sources.json`.

Citation: wwPDB Chemical Component Dictionary, ligand download service https://www.rcsb.org/downloads/ligands. No coordinate adjustment, atom/bond alteration, reformatting, optimization or new scientific calculation was performed. These records are independent molecules, not alternate conformers of the same molecule.
