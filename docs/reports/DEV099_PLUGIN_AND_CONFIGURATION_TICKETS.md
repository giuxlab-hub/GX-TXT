Loaded providers without a certified lifecycle truthfully require restart. CORE does not infer safe unloading from a filename or namespace and never silently forces a live editor, worker or scientific Job to stop. Provider callbacks own their own code/state removal and rollback; a failed callback is reported explicitly, including a failed rollback reason. Previous package backups remain outside discovery for pending runtime recovery. The hook is a host-only extension of the existing installer/loader authority; no additional scientific API, scheduler or persistence authority is created.
