family/curve/label/order roles. Its combined Dataset has193points, six curves and
actual bounds X `0.02004552627012056..4.968627360303313` V and Y
`0.020994681085760206..3.717917911127186` mA. Another calibration includes a small
negative Y. No available source table in that Session contains the task's cited
negative X minimum. The original Session was not changed to match an example.

The cited signed extent X `-0.45968..5.4478`, Y `-0.35007..4.0785` was therefore
tested through a separately published canonical fixture in the real Tcl/Tk GUI,
alongside all other signed/magnitude cases. Independent receipts verify complete
finite source coverage and exact grid/label coordinates. Evidence for the actual
supplied Dataset and the signed fixture is explicitly distinguished. A question
requesting the other source location was left available; no missing source result
was fabricated.
